Privacy policy

This policy describes Jun Li's private OpenClaw Family Assistant, including the Google OAuth application named openclaw. It does not describe other installations or the official OpenClaw project's services.

1. Operator and audience

Jun Li operates the assistant for personal and family use. Access is limited to individually authorized accounts and personally known users. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to openclawli67@gmail.com.

2. Information accessed

  • Google account information and credentials: the account identity returned by the connected API, granted permissions, OAuth access tokens, and refresh tokens.
  • Gmail: message identifiers, senders, recipients, subjects, bodies, labels, and attachments where a configured workflow retrieves them. Relevant content can include bills and transaction notices.
  • Google Calendar: authorized calendar and event information, including titles, times, descriptions, and related fields.
  • Google Drive and Sheets: authorized file metadata, search results, document content, and spreadsheet contents requested by a user or configured workflow.
  • Assistant interactions: user messages, generated replies, extracted task or bill records, summaries, and operational or audit logs.

Only the accounts and permissions individually authorized through Google's consent process are used. Drive and Sheets access is read-only. Calendar writes require explicit operator approval.

3. How information is used

Information is used to perform the assistant's configured, user-facing functions: email classification and organization, bill identification, summaries and notifications, calendar lookups and approved event actions, document searches, spreadsheet reading, and troubleshooting those functions. Email organization may change labels, mark messages as read, archive messages, or apply other specifically configured actions.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used by this application to train or improve generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

4. AI and other service providers

Selected user requests and relevant retrieved content may be sent to configured AI services, including OpenAI and, when selected, Anthropic, to produce classifications, summaries, extractions, or answers. This can include relevant email, calendar, or document content. Their handling and retention of submitted content depend on the selected service, account terms, and settings. The operator must use providers and settings consistent with Google's user-data policies.

Google processes API and authorization requests. Requested reports and replies may pass through Apple iMessage or other configured messaging services, such as Telegram, and are delivered to the configured recipients. These recipients may include authorized family members; users should consider the audience when requesting a report.

Private Google content is not published to this GitHub Pages website. It is not shared with unrelated parties for their own purposes. Disclosure may also occur where required by applicable law or necessary to investigate a security incident.

5. Storage and retention

OAuth credentials and selected application records are stored on the operator's computer. Local records may include message history, summaries, extracted bills or tasks, operational logs, audit receipts, and backups. AI and messaging providers may retain content under their applicable terms and settings.

There is no single automatic deletion period covering every local record. Records are retained while needed for the configured workflows, audit, or recovery, or until removed by the operator. Revoking Google access stops future authorized API access but does not automatically delete already stored records or backups.

6. Protection and access

The installation relies on Google's OAuth process, operating-system account and filesystem protections, and the assistant's configured access restrictions. Credentials must not be placed in this public website or repository. No system can guarantee absolute security; the operator is responsible for maintaining the computer, access controls, and backups.

7. Your choices and deletion requests

You can refuse requested permissions or revoke access at Google Account connections. Removing permissions can disable related functions.

To stop a workflow or request access to, correction of, or deletion of stored assistant records, contact openclawli67@gmail.com. Requests require reasonable identity verification. Backup copies and provider-held records may require separate handling; the operator will explain any relevant limits. This policy does not authorize deletion of family photos, videos, or calendar events.

8. This public website

This informational website has no sign-in form, advertising, analytics scripts, or assistant database connection. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical request information, including IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. External links are subject to their respective providers' policies.

9. Google API Limited Use

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

10. Policy updates

Changes to this policy will be published here with a revised effective date. Material changes to data use must be communicated to affected users, with new consent obtained where required.

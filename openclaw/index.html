OpenClaw Family Assistant

A personal assistant for everyday family administration, running on the operator's own computer.

What the application does

OpenClaw helps authorized users review email, identify bills, summarize relevant information, look up calendar events, and search documents and spreadsheets. Selected reports can be delivered through the family's configured messaging channels.

This installation uses the Google OAuth application named openclaw. Google accounts are connected individually with their owners' consent. The application is intended for the operator and personally known, authorized family users, not public registration.

Google account access

  • Gmail: read and classify email, identify bills, and apply configured labels, read status, and archive actions.
  • Google Calendar: read schedules and prepare event proposals. Creating, changing, or deleting events requires the operator's explicit approval.
  • Google Drive and Sheets: search and read authorized files and spreadsheet contents. These integrations use read-only access.

The permissions requested depend on the account and configured workflow. Access can be revoked through Google Account connections.

Privacy and AI processing

Selected content may be sent to configured AI providers to perform requested classifications, summaries, or answers. Processing, storage, messaging, retention, and user controls are explained in the privacy policy.

This public website contains application information only. It does not connect to a mailbox, collect Google credentials, or provide access to the private assistant.
